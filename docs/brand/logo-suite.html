1 · Primary Mark — "Node & Orbit"

PRIMARY — LIGHT BG
REVERSED — DARK BG

Construction: a teal sphere (the wallet — your keys, your node) holding a gold orbital ring clipped by the sphere's silhouette (the Lightning payment channel wrapping around it). Two white arc terminals on the ring's edge are the payment entry/exit points — read at a glance as "in" and "out". Concept preserved from Kongzi's 3D candidate (sphere + glowing ring), rebuilt flat so it survives favicon, emboss, and single-color print.

2 · Monochrome & Small Size

120mm → 16px. Below 32px the arc terminals drop out; sphere + gold ring still read. The ring-vs-sphere separation is geometry (clipped edge), not color — so mono embossing on cards keeps the form.

3 · Horizontal Lockup & Wordmark

openLN

6 · Design Rationale

Sphere = the wallet. Self-custodial, complete, sovereign — the core. Gold ring = Lightning: a channel that orbits the wallet rather than passing through a custodian. The clip is literal: the payment path is defined by the wallet's boundary, not the platform's. Terminals = receive/send, the two directions of every openLN transaction.
Polish, not copying: institutional-grade restraint (two colors, one accent, strict hierarchy) applied to a Bitcoin-native identity. Deep teal grounds it in finance; gold signals the Lightning layer without wearing Bitcoin-orange cosplay.
