The solution

Type amount.
Scan or Tap.

The RIC is dialed in, so when the moment comes to accept a Bitcoin payment, the process is simple - no more errors or awkward “How do I do this again?” moments. The RIC removes that friction by making the process easy, consistent and intuitive every time.

2-way functionality means the RIC can send as well as receive sats, so merchants can also choose to use it as a portal for physical trades; buy and sell Bitcoin for cash in person without any extra setup.

  1. 01

    Staff type the amount.

    Keypad and screen always ready. Same as any card terminal.

  2. 02

    Customer taps a card or scans.

    An openLN card, any Bolt Card, or any Lightning wallet by QR.

  3. 03

    Sats land in the merchant's wallet.

    Their wallet, their keys. The same terminal also writes and wipes NTAG424 cards at the counter.

Cashflow

A route,
not a vault.

openLN routes the payment and requires a routing fee. It does not hold balances. The customer's sats are locked to a hold invoice, forwarded to the merchant's own wallet, and settled in one motion.

Your wallet connects over Nostr Wallet Connect (NIP-47). openLN never holds your keys, and there is no openLN balance to withdraw. Payments pass through, only the routing fee is left behind.
